Straight answers

What nobody dares to ask.

What do you charge?

A percentage of the final sale price, agreed with you before anything is signed. Which means: if your home does not sell, you pay nothing. No joining fee, no advertising costs, nothing to pay up front.

It is already advertised and nobody calls. What changes?

It is almost always the price, and sometimes the photographs. At the valuation we tell you which of the two it is. If it is only the photographs, we will say so anyway and you can fix it with the agency you already have.

Am I tied into a sole agency?

We work both ways. We explain what your property gains from each and you decide.

Is the valuation really free?

Yes. We come to your home, we see it, and we give you a price based on what has sold on your street. We charge you nothing and you commit to nothing.

How long does a home take to sell in the Maresme?

It depends on the street and on the asking price. A well priced home moves in weeks. A badly priced one can sit for a year, and it sells in the end anyway: for less than it was worth on day one.

Do you speak English?

Yes. And català and castellano.
