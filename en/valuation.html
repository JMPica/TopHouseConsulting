Online valuation

What about the calculator on the website?

Six details and we give you a range straight away. It will come out wide, and that is deliberate: any website that gives you an exact figure without having seen your home is making it up.

This is an automatic calculation based on market prices in your area. It cannot see your exact street, the floor, the light or the real condition, and those four things move the price by thousands of euros. To know what it is really worth, someone has to go and look.
