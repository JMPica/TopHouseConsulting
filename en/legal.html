Privacy policy

Who processes your data

The data controller is Top House Real Estate, S.L., with tax number B06925135, registered office at Riera del Bisbe Pol, 56, 08350 Arenys de Mar (Barcelona), telephone 605 27 31 50 and email info@tophouserealestate.es.

What data we collect, and only when you write to us

This website does not ask you for any data in order to view it. There is no registration, no account and no tracking. There are only two moments when you give us information, and in both because you choose to:

  • The valuation calculator. The property details (type, town, area, floor area, condition, rooms) are calculated inside your own browser and are not sent anywhere. If you do not carry on, they never leave your device.
  • The contact form. Here we do: your name, your phone number, the message you write and, depending on which form you use, the area the property is in and what you need, or the reference of the property you would like to view.

How it reaches us: by email and by WhatsApp

This matters, and you should know it before pressing the button, because sending the form does two things at once.

It reaches us by email. Your details pass through our server, which turns them into an email to our inbox. That email is all that remains: the form is not stored in any database on this website. All the server writes to disk is how many enquiries arrive per hour, and it does so without storing your IP address: it turns it into a code, uses it to count, and deletes it after an hour. It is there to stop automated junk and nothing else.

And WhatsApp opens. With the message already drafted, so that you decide whether to send it. If you do send it, those same details also travel through WhatsApp, which is a service of Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, and they are subject to its terms and its privacy policy, including any international data transfers Meta may make. We do not control that processing. If you close WhatsApp without sending anything, your enquiry has already reached us by email.

If you would rather not use WhatsApp, you have two alternatives that do not involve Meta: call us on 605 27 31 50 or come to the office.

What we use your data for

  • To get in touch with you and answer what you ask us.
  • To prepare and carry out the valuation of your home, if that is what you requested.
  • To manage the relationship if you go on to instruct us for the sale or the letting.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not send you newsletters you have not asked for and we do not share or sell your data to third parties.

Our legal basis for processing

  • Your consent, given when you send us the message, so we can contact you and deal with your request.
  • Performance of a contract or steps taken before entering into one, if you instruct us for a service.
  • Legal obligations, such as tax obligations and anti-money-laundering rules, which in the property sector require the parties to be identified and documentation to be kept.

Who else sees it

Besides WhatsApp, as explained above, your data may be accessed by the providers who render us services and who are bound by contract to the same confidentiality: our estate agency CRM, our accountants, and our web and email hosting providers. Also the public authorities where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

For as long as the relationship lasts, and afterwards for as long as legal obligations require (for example, six years for commercial records and ten for anti-money-laundering records). If your enquiry comes to nothing, we delete your data once it is no longer needed.

Cookie policy

This website does not use cookies. Neither its own nor third-party ones. Nor does it store anything in your browser by other means.

That is why you will not see a cookie banner

The banner that pops up on almost every website exists because the law requires your permission before installing cookies that are not strictly necessary. Here there are none to install, so asking your permission would be asking for nothing. We would rather not bother you with a window that means nothing.

What we have deliberately avoided

  • No analytics. There is no Google Analytics and no visit counter. We do not know who comes in, from where, or what they look at.
  • No advertising pixels. There is no Meta pixel or anyone else's. Visiting this website does not mean our adverts will follow you around afterwards.
  • No external fonts. The typefaces are served from this same domain. Linking them from Google would send your IP address to Google without asking you, which is exactly what has been penalised in Europe.
  • No embedded maps or videos. The video on the home page is hosted here.

The result is that, while you browse this website, your browser does not talk to any server other than ours. You can check this yourself with your browser's developer tools, in the network tab.

If this were to change

If one day we add analytics, a chat widget or a map, this will no longer be true: the corresponding cookie banner will appear and this page will be updated first.
