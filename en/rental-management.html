What we do for you

From the advert to the signed contract.

  • We find the tenant

    We take care of finding the person who will rent your home, so you do not have to.

  • We check the tenant can pay before anyone signs

    Before you sign anything, we check that the tenant can pay the rent.

  • Contract and paperwork included

    We prepare the contract and take care of the rental paperwork. We also arrange habitability certificates, energy performance certificates and technical building inspections.

  • Guaranteed rent

    We explain in writing, before you sign, what it covers and on what terms.

Day to day

And the day-to-day management?

Every owner needs something different: some only want the tenant and the contract, others would rather not have to deal with anything else. Tell us what you need and we will set out in writing what the service includes and what it costs, before you sign anything.
