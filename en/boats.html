The instruction

What we do with a house. On the water.

Selling a boat on your own means months of calls, viewings that never buy and paperwork nobody explains. We take all of it off your hands.

  • If it does not sell, you do not pay

    A percentage of the final price, agreed with you before anything is signed. No upfront fee and no advertising costs.

  • We see it at its mooring

    We go to the marina and look at it inside and out, engine included. Nobody values a boat from a photo.

  • A price based on real sales

    We compare it with similar boats that have actually sold, not with asking prices online.

  • Only buyers who can afford it come aboard

    Photography, a full listing and screened viewings. Your Saturdays stay yours.

  • Paperwork and signing

    Contract, change of ownership and whatever the Harbour Master asks for. You sign, we handle the rest.

  • Moorings

    If you no longer need the mooring, we help you transfer it too. And if you are looking for one, we find it.

Straight answers

What people always ask us.

What boats do you sell?

Motor boats, sailing boats and leisure RIBs. If you have something different, ask us and we will tell you honestly whether it is for us.

Does it have to be in Arenys?

No. We work in the Maresme marinas: El Masnou, Port Balís, Mataró and Arenys de Mar.

What do you charge?

A percentage of the final sale price, agreed with you before anything is signed. Which means: if your boat does not sell, you do not pay.

And if I want to buy one?

Tell us what you are after, the length and the budget, and we will find it for you.
